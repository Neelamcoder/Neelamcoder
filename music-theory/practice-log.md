# My Practice Log

Add a new entry at the bottom each day after you practice. The 11 PM email
reads your most recent entries and builds that night's music theory lesson
around them.

Keep it short. Any of these help:

- **Instrument**: what you played
- **Practiced**: scales, songs, exercises, ragas, chords
- **Struggled with**: what felt hard or confusing
- **Question**: anything you want explained

Copy this template for each new day:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD
- Instrument:
- Practiced:
- Struggled with:
- Question:
```
